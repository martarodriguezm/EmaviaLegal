Privacy Policy — Emavia

November 2026

Overview

Emavia respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Emavia iOS application.

In short: your goals and your cycle data stay on your device. No accounts, no advertising, no selling of data. We only receive anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that help us keep the app working.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Emavia is designed to work on-device. The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Goals and goal-related data (titles, areas, progress, check-ins)
  • Cycle information, if you enable cycle mode: the start date of your last period, your typical cycle length and your typical period length
  • App settings (such as reminders and preferences)

This information is not sent to us or to any third party.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your Emavia data may be included in that backup, which is managed by Apple under your Apple Account.

2. Apple Health

Emavia can optionally read your menstruation records from Apple Health (HealthKit). This only happens if you choose to import from Apple Health and grant permission in the system prompt.

  • Access is read-only: Emavia does not write to or change your Health data
  • The data is used only to estimate the start of your last period and your typical cycle and period length, so the app can show your current cycle phase
  • It is processed on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party
  • It is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and it is never sold
  • You decide how much history to share, and you can revoke access at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → Emavia) or in iOS Settings

You can use Emavia without Apple Health by entering your cycle details manually, or by turning cycle mode off.

3. Personal Data

Emavia does not require account creation.

We do not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Your goals, check-ins or cycle information
  • Your Apple Health data

Any information you enter into the app is stored locally and is fully under your control.

4. Usage Statistics and Crash Reports

To understand how the app is used and to fix problems, Emavia collects limited technical information that is not linked to your identity:

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): for example, app opens and screens viewed, together with general device information such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate region
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): technical details about a crash, such as the device model, iOS version and the part of the app that failed

This information never includes your goals, check-ins, cycle information or Apple Health data. It is not used for advertising and Emavia does not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Emavia may send local notifications to your device.

  • Notifications are generated locally on your device
  • No notification data is sent to external services
  • You can disable notifications at any time in the app or system settings

6. Payments and Subscriptions

Emavia offers optional paid features.

  • Payments are processed securely through Apple’s App Store
  • Emavia does not have access to your payment information
  • Subscription management is handled by Apple
  • To know whether your subscription is active, Emavia uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history for Emavia and an anonymous identifier generated by the app. It does not receive your name, email or any data you enter into the app

7. Third-Party Services

Emavia uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple — in-app purchases, subscriptions and App Store analytics
  • RevenueCat — subscription status
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) — usage statistics and crash reports

None of these services receives your goals, check-ins, cycle information or Apple Health data. Each provider processes the information described above under its own privacy policy.

8. Data Retention and Your Choices

Data stored by Emavia remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

  • You can turn cycle mode off at any time in the app’s settings
  • You can revoke Emavia’s access to Apple Health at any time
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are retained by the providers listed above for a limited period, according to their own policies

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as accessing or deleting it. Because Emavia has no accounts and your content stays on your device, we usually hold nothing that identifies you, but you can contact us with any request.

9. Not Medical Advice

Emavia estimates cycle phases to help you plan. It is not a contraceptive method, a fertility tool or medical advice, and it should not replace professional guidance.

10. Children’s Privacy

Emavia is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page, with an updated “Last updated” date.
