Privacy Policy – Emavia

Last updated February 2026

Overview

Emavia respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Emavia iOS application.

In short: your data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no selling of data.

1. Information We Collect

Emavia is designed to work primarily offline and on-device.

We may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Goals and goal-related data (titles, areas, progress, check-ins)
  • Cycle-related preferences (if enabled by the user)
  • App settings (such as reminders and preferences)

This data is not transmitted to external servers and remains on your device.

2. Personal Data

Emavia does not require account creation.

We do not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Health data synced to Apple Health

Any information you enter into the app is stored locally and is fully under your control.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Emavia may send local notifications to your device.

  • Notifications are generated locally on your device
  • No notification data is sent to external services
  • You can disable notifications at any time in the app or system settings

4. Payments and Subscriptions

Emavia may offer optional paid features.

  • Payments are processed securely through Apple’s App Store
  • Emavia does not have access to your payment information
  • Subscription management is handled by Apple

5. Third-Party Services

Emavia may use third-party services to support optional features, such as:

  • In-app purchases and subscriptions (via Apple)
  • Analytics provided by Apple (App Store analytics)

No third-party service receives personal user data entered into the app.

6. Data Retention

All data stored by Emavia remains on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app.

Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.

7. Children’s Privacy

Emavia is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page, with an updated “Last updated” date.
